Assignment view — modernization (v1 mock, post-review)

Four states. Every state renders one page header (no tab strip), a main region and a rail region per panel-layout-pattern.md: regions draw nothing, each block is one PanelSection, collapse is a container query with the rail last, colours are tokens only. Structure reflects the deepseek-v4-pro:0813 review: materials sit in the rail as Content & Resources, the panel name Targets & Audience is kept, and the primary action follows the settled §11 matrix.

What to check

  • One title, one action pair. Published → primary Open progress, with Unpublish and Close in the kebab (§11 view-surface exception, Q141). Draft → primary Publish, gated by approval.
  • Delivery is the new panel: cohort by student, delivery pill per contact, a dashed not reached row.
  • Content & Resources lives in the rail and carries the grounding readout — no separate AI panel.

Delivery vocabulary — one rule set, five variants

  • sent handed to the channel · opened the recipient followed the deep link
  • queued due now or waiting on a retry · retry 16:20 failed, retry scheduled
  • no more retries terminal failure · skipped suppressed by policy, not an error
  • not reached a student with no subscribed contact — the row the current tabs cannot show
